Stripe / Yellow Rust (पीला रतुआ)

Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici

Confidence: 94.2%
Observed Symptoms
  • Yellow to orange-yellow powdery pustules arranged in linear stripes on leaf surfaces
  • Chlorosis and drying of infected leaf tissues spreading rapidly under high humidity
Crop-Care Guidance
  • Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1ml/L (200 ml/acre) immediately
  • Avoid excess nitrogen top-dressing which fosters fungal proliferation
Banda Weather Context: Overcast skies with night temperatures between 10-15°C and relative humidity >70% in Banda provide ideal conditions for rust sporulation.
Recommended Next Action: Intimate local Agriculture Department officer and check PMFBY localized pest damage guidelines.

What is PMFBY Coverage?

Comprehensive crop insurance scheme from sowing to post-harvest against unavoidable non-preventable natural risks (hailstorms, floods, unseasonal rain, pests).

Crucial 72-Hour Claim Window

1. Calamity Occurs

Hailstorm, inundation, unseasonal rainfall, or localized landslide affects your standing crop.

2. Intimate within 72 Hours

Inform Agriculture Insurance Company / Bank / CSC / Crop Insurance App within 72 hours of damage.

3. Survey & Assessment

Joint committee conducts GPS-tagged loss assessment within 10 days.
