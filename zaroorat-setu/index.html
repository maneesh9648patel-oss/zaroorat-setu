🎯 Official HackFest 3.0 Problem Statement
“How might we build an inclusive, low-cost community platform that connects people who need essential resources with verified individuals and organizations that can provide them, using urgency-based matching, offline request access, and reusable resource coordination?”
Real-World Scenarios

The Problem

Critical everyday resource bottlenecks across students, families, and patients in Tier-2 and Tier-3 communities:

🎓
Education

Student Fees & Scholarships

A student cannot pay fees, or lacks accurate scholarship information.

Crucial semester deadlines or examination fees get missed simply because authentic financial aid schemes and textbook sharing never reach the student in time.
🩺
Medical

Temporary Equipment Needs

A family needs a wheelchair or medical equipment for a few days, but buying is costly.

Post-operative recovery frequently requires crutches, a walker, or a wheelchair for just 2–3 weeks. Purchasing costs ₹6,000–₹15,000, while dozens sit idle in closets nearby.
📦
Surplus Items

Unused Everyday Reusables

Extra books, clothes and household items go unused because nobody knows who needs them.

Graduating students and households hold scientific calculators, drafting tools, warm blankets, and textbooks with zero trusted local channels to pass them forward.
🚨
Emergencies

Time-Critical Contact Delays

Blood donor, transport or local volunteer needed, but finding the right contact takes time.

Informal chat groups get flooded with spam, outdated donor numbers, and forwarded rumors right when minutes matter most for patient transit and blood transfusions.
💡
Connect these needs through a local, verified, priority-based network that supports donation alongside temporary lending, services and verified public-resource information.
The Mechanism

Our Solution / How It Works

A frictionless three-step loop designed for both digital-native and feature-phone users:

01 📲

Post a need

(app, SMS or missed call)

Submit your request through our lightweight web app, or dial a toll-free missed call or text SMS on any basic phone. Local student and civic volunteers assist in registration without internet barriers.

02 🤝

Get verified and matched

(human reviewer confirms; AI suggests priority; nearby provider found)

Our AI Need-Priority Engine assesses severity, time constraints, and available resources. A trusted local human verifier makes the final validation, and the system connects a nearby verified helper.

03 🔄

Receive and reuse

(QR handover; borrowed items return to the network)

Contact numbers stay masked until mutual agreement. Physical exchanges are authenticated with quick QR scans. Borrowed equipment returns via automated reminders, creating a perpetual community pool.

Core Innovations

What Makes It Genuinely Different

Four targeted technical and operational capabilities built specifically for grassroots reliability:

🧠
A

AI Need-Priority Engine

Priority is suggested from urgency, severity of need, available resources and waiting time, not first-come-first-served. A human verifier makes the final decision.

⚡ Urgency-Weighted Triage
📶
B

Access Without Internet

Feature-phone users can submit requests by missed call, SMS or assisted registration by a volunteer.

📱 Zero-Data Intake
🔁
C

Borrow, Return, Reuse

Wheelchairs, calculators, books and tools can be borrowed temporarily, with QR handover and return reminders, so one resource serves many people.

🔄 Circular Asset Sharing
🛡️
D

Verified Need, Verified Help

Verification, duplicate detection and completed-help confirmation reduce fake and repeated requests. Personal details are shared only as needed, after both sides agree.

Civic Ethics

Responsible Design

Safety, human accountability, and privacy protocols engineered directly into the platform:

🔒

Mutual Consent Privacy

Personal phone numbers and home addresses are never exposed to public feeds or web crawlers. Contact details are shared only as needed, after both sides agree.

👥

Human-in-the-Loop AI

The AI urgency engine suggests a priority rank based on clinical urgency and timeline, but a local human verifier makes the final decision before allocation.

🛡️

Fraud & Duplicate Shield

Fuzzy matching, phone tokenization, and loan-cycle limits automatically flag duplicate requests and prevent hoarding without collecting intrusive personal biometrics.

♿

Low-Literacy & Feature Phone Access

Built with high-contrast text, clear visual icons, audio voice prompts, and direct missed-call/SMS intake for users without smartphones or digital literacy.
