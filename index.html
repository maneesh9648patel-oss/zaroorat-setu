🩺 Immediate Golden-Hour First Aid Guide
LIFE SAVING
Top Priority

Arterial & Severe Bleeding Protocol

01
Direct Firm Pressure: Apply immediate, direct two-handed pressure on the wound using a clean cloth, jacket, or sterile dressing. Do not release pressure.
02
Elevate Injured Limb: If an arm or leg is bleeding and there are no evident bone fractures, raise it above heart level while maintaining firm pressure.
03
Tourniquet Application: For catastrophic limb hemorrhage, place a tourniquet 2 to 3 inches above the wound (never directly on a joint) and twist until bleeding halts. Note the application time.
Cardiac Arrest

Hands-Only CPR Protocol & Metronome

01
Check Responsiveness: Shake victim's shoulder and shout. Check for normal breathing for no more than 10 seconds.
02
Center of Chest: Place the heel of one hand in the center of the chest (lower half of breastbone), lock fingers with the other hand.
03
Push Hard & Fast: Compress 2 inches (5 cm) deep at 100-120 compressions per minute until paramedics take over. Use the metronome below for exact rhythm.

Critical Precaution

Spinal & Neck Trauma Safety

01
DO NOT MOVE THE VICTIM: Unless there is an imminent fire, explosion, or submersion risk, NEVER move an accident victim. Moving can sever spinal cord nerves and cause permanent paralysis.
02
Stabilize Head & Neck: Kneel behind the victim's head and hold their head steady in line with their spine with both hands to prevent any twisting or tilting.
03
Motorcycle Helmets: DO NOT remove a motorcyclist's helmet unless they are not breathing and CPR is impossible with the helmet on.
Explosion Hazard

Vehicle Fire & Fuel Leak Evacuation

01
Turn Off Ignition: If safely accessible, switch off the car engine immediately to cut electrical fuel pumps and spark hazards.
02
100-Foot Perimeter: Evacuate all conscious occupants at least 100 feet (30 meters) away from the vehicle upwind to avoid toxic smoke inhalation.
03
Never Open Hood: If smoke or flames emerge from under the hood, DO NOT open the hood fully. Rushing oxygen can cause an explosive fireball flare.
